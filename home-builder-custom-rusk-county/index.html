What the build covers and how to assess it

A custom home project can involve planning the work, selecting materials, construction, framing and finish carpentry. The details depend on the home and the agreed scope. Keep these points in mind as you compare providers:

  • Scope: Ask for a clear description of the work, materials and decisions included in the estimate.
  • Timing: A project schedule depends on its size, scope, material selections and construction sequence. Discuss timing with the builder once the project details are clearer.
  • Cost factors: The property, home design, material choices, finish level and amount of work all affect the overall cost. KRL discusses your budget while developing the scope and estimate.
  • Provider comparison: Compare how each builder explains the work, handles open questions and documents what is included. A similar-sounding estimate may cover different items.

You may hear “spec home,” meaning a home built for sale, or “build-to-rent,” meaning a home planned for rental use. “Finish carpentry” refers to detailed interior woodwork and trim. Knowing these terms makes it easier to discuss what your project needs.
